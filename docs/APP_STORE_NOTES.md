### Full Description

**Tired of worrying about online marketplace scams?**

ScamSight is your personal scam risk analyzer for Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, OfferUp, and other online listings. Get instant risk scores, red flag detection, and personalized safety checklists before you buy.

**How It Works:**
1. Paste any listing URL
2. Our guided wizard helps you capture key details (no scraping!)
3. Get an instant risk score (0-100) with explainable breakdown
4. Review tailored safety tips and red flags

**Privacy-First Design:**
✓ All data stays on your device by default
✓ No account required
✓ No tracking or selling of your information
✓ Photos analyzed locally (optional cloud features require opt-in)

**Key Features:**
• **Risk Scoring:** Deterministic algorithm analyzes seller signals, pricing, red flags, and more
• **User-Assisted Capture:** We don't scrape—you manually confirm details for compliance and accuracy
• **Local Image Analysis:** Detect reused photos across listings (on-device perceptual hashing)
• **Market Comparisons:** See if the price is too good to be true
• **Safety Checklists:** Get personalized tips based on risk level
• **History Tracking:** Save and review past analyses
• **Dark Mode:** Fully supports system theme

**What ScamSight Doesn't Do:**
✗ We don't guarantee listings are safe (use your judgment!)
✗ We don't scrape or access marketplace websites without permission
✗ We don't replace common sense and due diligence

**Perfect For:**
- Cautious online shoppers
- Parents buying for kids
- First-time marketplace users
- Anyone who wants peace of mind

Download ScamSight and shop online with confidence!

## App Review Notes

**Important Compliance Information:**

1. **No Scraping:**
   - ScamSight does NOT scrape websites or bypass access controls
   - We use user-assisted capture: the app opens listings in an in-app browser, and users manually enter details
   - This complies with marketplace terms of service

2. **Privacy:**
   - All user data is stored locally in SQLite
   - External services (reverse image search, eBay API) are optional and require explicit opt-in
   - No tracking, no account system, no personal data collection

3. **Permissions:**
   - Camera/Photos: Optional, for capturing listing photos (analyzed locally)
   - Internet: Only for optional features (market comps, reverse image search if enabled)

4. **Demo Account:**
   - No account system—app works entirely offline/locally
   - To test: Paste any URL (e.g., a Facebook Marketplace listing URL) and walk through the wizard

5. **Testing Instructions:**
   - Open the app
   - Tap "Analyze Listing" on Home screen
   - Enter a test URL (e.g., `https://facebook.com/marketplace/item/123`)
   - Walk through the 4-step wizard
   - View the risk report

**This app does NOT violate any platform's terms of service.**
