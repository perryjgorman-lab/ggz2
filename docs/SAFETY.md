# Safety Guidelines - ScamSight

ScamSight helps you assess scam risk, but it's not foolproof. Follow these guidelines to stay safe when buying from online marketplaces.

## 🛡️ General Safety Rules

### Before Meeting

1. **Research the Seller**
   - Check their profile history, reviews, and account age
   - Search their name/phone number for scam reports
   - Be wary of new accounts with high-value items

2. **Verify the Item**
   - Ask for additional photos, videos, or serial numbers
   - Research the item's market value
   - If it seems too good to be true, it probably is

3. **Communicate Through the Platform**
   - Keep all communication on the marketplace platform
   - Avoid moving to text/email/WhatsApp early
   - Platform chat provides a record if disputes arise

### During the Transaction

4. **Meet Safely**
   - Choose a public, well-lit location (police station parking lots are ideal)
   - Bring a friend or family member
   - Meet during daylight hours
   - Trust your instincts—leave if something feels wrong

5. **Inspect Thoroughly**
   - Test the item before paying (if applicable)
   - Check for damage, missing parts, or authenticity issues
   - For electronics: verify IMEI, serial numbers, functionality

6. **Payment Methods**
   - Prefer cash for local transactions
   - Use platform payment systems when available (buyer protection)
   - **NEVER** use wire transfers, cryptocurrency, or gift cards
   - Avoid Venmo/Zelle for high-value items (no buyer protection)

### After the Transaction

7. **Keep Records**
   - Save all messages, receipts, and photos
   - Note the seller's contact information
   - Report suspicious activity to the platform

## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

### Immediate Deal-Breakers
- Seller refuses to meet in person (shipping only)
- Requests payment via wire transfer, crypto, or gift cards
- Asks for personal information (SSN, passwords, etc.)
- Pressures you to "act now" or uses urgency tactics
- Price is drastically below market value

### Warning Signs
- New account selling expensive items
- Poor grammar/spelling (possible automated scam)
- Stock photos or watermarked images
- Seller wants to move off-platform quickly
- Vague or copy-pasted item descriptions
- Seller has no reviews or negative feedback

## 📱 Platform-Specific Tips

### Facebook Marketplace
- Use "Facebook Marketplace Checkout" for buyer protection
- Check seller's Facebook profile (age, friends, activity)
- Look for "Badge of Trust" (verified sellers)
- Report suspicious listings to Facebook

### Craigslist
- Meet in person (Craigslist has no buyer protection)
- Beware of "shipping" offers (almost always scams)
- Ignore email scams (fake PayPal confirmations, etc.)
- Use cash and inspect items carefully

### OfferUp
- Use "TruYou" verified sellers when possible
- Use OfferUp's in-app payment for protection
- Meet at designated OfferUp Community MeetUp Spots

## 💡 What ScamSight Can and Can't Do

### ScamSight CAN Help:
✅ Identify common scam patterns (new account, suspicious payment, etc.)
✅ Calculate risk based on multiple signals
✅ Detect reused photos across listings (local analysis)
✅ Compare prices to market averages
✅ Provide a structured safety checklist

### ScamSight CANNOT:
❌ Guarantee a listing is legitimate
❌ Verify seller identity
❌ Prevent all scams
❌ Replace your own judgment

**Always use ScamSight as one tool among many, not your sole decision-maker.**

## 🆘 If You've Been Scammed

1. **Contact the Platform**
   - Report the listing and seller immediately
   - Request a refund if you used platform payment

2. **Contact Your Bank**
   - Report fraudulent charges to your bank/card issuer
   - Request a chargeback if applicable

3. **File a Police Report**
   - Especially for high-value items or identity theft
   - Keep a copy for insurance/legal purposes

4. **Report to Authorities**
   - **FTC:** [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov)
   - **IC3 (Cybercrime):** [ic3.gov](https://ic3.gov)
   - **BBB Scam Tracker:** [bbb.org/scamtracker](https://www.bbb.org/scamtracker)

5. **Warn Others**
   - Leave reviews on the platform
   - Share your experience on scam-reporting sites
